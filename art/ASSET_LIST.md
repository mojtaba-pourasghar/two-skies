- **#۱ کایرو (انجام‌شده):** character design sheet, 17-year-old boy Kairo, short messy dark hair, short orange-red scarf with white stitch line, dark jacket with orange trim, fingerless gloves, glowing blue hack module on left forearm, front/side/back views, small sprite proportions
- **#۲ ایلارا (انجام‌شده):** character design sheet, 16-year-old girl Ilara, shoulder-length dark hair with small braid, long flowing turquoise scarf, amber jacket, tall boots, notebook at hip, seed bow on back, front/side/back
- **#۶ تنهٔ درخت (انجام‌شده):** giant fallen tree trunk bridge over deep turquoise water, mossy boulder cliffs, large jungle leaves in corners, glowing blue crystals in the water
- **#۷ فلات:** grassy plateau on a tall rounded-boulder cliff, dirt path, pine trees, waterfall with mist on the left, distant blue-violet mesas, white clouds below
- **#۸ غار شبانه:** night cave, wall torches with warm orange halo, thin rope bridge with wooden pegs, dark vignette, glowing blue crystals
- **#۹ شهر:** flat-roofed sand-colored town on a bay, hanging lanterns, barrels and pots, ropes between roofs, wooden dock, turquoise water
- **#۱۰ خلیج:** shallow turquoise bay, pink and purple coral, small waterfall, white foam line on the rocks
- **#۱۱ سیاه‌چال:** dark green-teal dungeon, wall torches, cobwebs, thick wooden beams, pink-white creatures standing out against the dark
- **#۱۹ مقدمه:** two siblings under two skies, one warm earth sky and one glowing aura sky, wide painterly vista
